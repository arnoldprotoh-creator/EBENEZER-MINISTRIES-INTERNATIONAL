About Us

A Place Where Hope Is Restored

Ebenezer Ministries International is a vibrant, Christ-centered church family committed to spreading the love of God and the message of salvation across nations. Our name, Ebenezer stone of help reminds us that in every season, our help comes from the Lord.

We exist to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ through worship, teaching, fellowship, and service to our community and the world.
